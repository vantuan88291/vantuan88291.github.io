Summary: SubGlance translates text on your screen, camera, and subtitles. Your privacy is paramount—we process translations on-device whenever possible and do not collect or sell your personal data.

2. Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the SubGlance application.

A. Information Processing and Data Handling

  • On-Device Processing: Features like Screen Area Translation, Camera Translation, Subtitle Translation, and Text Translation perform Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and text processing locally on your device whenever supported.
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • No Data Tracking: Your translation history and captured images remain solely on your device unless you choose to export or clear them. We do not track your usage or translation content.

B. Device Permissions

To provide core functionalities, SubGlance may request access to the following permissions on your device:

  • Camera Permission: Required solely for Camera Translation mode to capture live text from your surroundings. Camera streams are processed in real-time and are never stored or transmitted externally.
  • Screen Overlay / Accessibility Services: Required to enable floating translation widgets and read screen text for Screen Area and Subtitle translation features.
  • Storage / Media Access: Optional, used only if you explicitly choose to import files (e.g., subtitle files or image files) for translation.

C. Third-Party Services & Licenses

SubGlance uses open-source libraries and machine-learning frameworks to enable OCR and translation capabilities. These tools operate strictly within the app's local environment and do not transmit user data to external servers.

3. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit any information from anyone under the age of 13. SubGlance is designed as a general utility tool suitable for all audiences.
