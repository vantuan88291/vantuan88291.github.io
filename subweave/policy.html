1. What stays on your device

  • Translation runs on your device. SubWeave uses the Tencent HY-MT 1.5 translation model (“Best” mode), which is downloaded once and then runs locally through llama.cpp, and Google ML Kit on-device translation (“Fast” mode). Google ML Kit language identification also runs on the device, to detect the language of a subtitle file.
  • The subtitle files you open, their translations, your edits and your glossary are processed and stored on your device. They are not sent to us or to any third party. Files are opened and saved only through the system file picker or Share that you choose.
  • Your settings, glossary and translation job history are stored only on your device. You can clear them in the app or by clearing the app's data.

2. Data collected by third-party services

We don't operate our own analytics or ad servers and don't sell personal data. The following Google services are built into the app. Their data is linked to app or device identifiers, never to your name or account.

ServiceDataPurpose
Firebase Crashlytics Crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, Android version, app version, a Crashlytics installation ID, and counts and identifiers such as the number of lines and the language pair. Never subtitle text, translations, glossary terms or file names. Find and fix crashes
Google Analytics for Firebase App usage events (for example a file was opened, a translation started or finished with counts grouped in ranges, the language pair, a file was saved or shared) and an app instance ID. Never text or file names. Understand which features and languages are used
Google AdMob (only while ads are enabled) Advertising ID, device information, IP address, ad interactions. Ads may be personalised only with your consent. Show ads that fund the app
Google User Messaging Platform (only while ads are enabled) Your consent choices, where a consent form is required (for example in the EEA and the UK). Record ad and privacy choices

These services act under Google's terms and policies: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps that use its services · Firebase privacy.

About ads: SubWeave is free and shows ads to fund its development: a banner and occasional full-screen or rewarded ads. Ads can be turned off remotely, without an app update. While they are off, the ads service isn't started and collects nothing.

3. When the app uses the internet

  • Translation model download: once, through Google Play's asset delivery or, if that isn't available, from Hugging Face.
  • ML Kit language packs: Google ML Kit downloads language packs from Google the first time a language is used in Fast mode.
  • App configuration: the app downloads a small settings file from a Cloudflare Workers address. It sends nothing about you beyond the standard web request; like any web request, the server sees your IP address, which we don't use or store.
  • Google Play: in-app update and review prompts.
  • Crash reports, usage statistics and, while enabled, ads (section 2).

Translation itself works offline once the model is installed.

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for the downloads, ads and reporting in section 3.
  • Notifications (asked during onboarding): shows the progress of a translation and a “translation finished” notification.
  • Foreground service (data sync) and wake lock: keep a translation running while the app is in the background.
  • Run at startup: used only to tell you that a translation was interrupted by a restart. It does not start translating by itself.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID / Ad Services): used for ads while they are enabled.

SubWeave does not request storage, camera, microphone, location or contacts permissions. Files are opened and saved only through the system file picker or Share that you choose.

5. Your choices and data deletion

  • Ads (while enabled): you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads). Where a consent form applies, you can change your choice again in the app under Settings › About › Privacy options.
  • Data on your device: delete job history, files and glossary entries in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
  • Backup: the downloaded model, job history and app configuration are excluded from Android backup. Your glossary and settings may be included in your Android backup.
  • Data held by Google services: it isn't linked to your identity. To ask us to delete analytics or crash data related to your device, email us (section 9) with the approximate date. We'll handle it through the tools these services provide.

6. Children

SubWeave is a general translation tool and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we'll take appropriate steps.
