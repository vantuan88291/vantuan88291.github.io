Features

Built for books

Translate, read and save your EPUB and TXT books without leaving your phone.

01

Whole books, on the device

Translates EPUB and TXT books on your phone. Your books never leave it. EPUB output keeps chapters, images and styling. For TXT, the app detects the text encoding and splits it into chapters.

How it works

Ready in three steps

Open a book

Pick an EPUB or TXT file, or send it to SubLeaf with Share. DRM-protected books are not supported; SubLeaf opens DRM-free EPUB and TXT only.

Choose Best or Fast

Best for natural wording, Fast for a near-instant result.

Read, fix and save

Read while it translates, fix any paragraph, then save a new file or share it.

Privacy

Private by design

  • On-device. Your books, translations, glossary and reading progress stay on your phone. Books are never uploaded.
  • No account. No sign-up and no sign-in.
  • Free, with ads. The app may show ads (Google AdMob).
  • Limited network use. The internet is used for downloading the translation model and language packs, app settings updates, ads, and crash reports and usage statistics. See the Privacy Policy.
