What it does

Subtitles

Live translation of the subtitles in any video app, shown right over the original line, with optional voice-over.

Screen area

Drop a frame on games, comics, chats or menus and translate exactly what's inside it.

Text

Type or paste text, or translate a selection from any app, and listen to the result.

Camera

Point at signs, menus and documents, or pick a photo, and brush over the lines you want translated.

Private by design

  • Recognition and translation run on your device. Your text, screen and photos are never uploaded.
  • No account and no sign-in.
  • The app uses the internet only for the one-time model download, app settings updates, and anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. See the Privacy Policy.
