1. What stays on your device

  • Reading and translation run on your device. SubInk reads the text on a page and translates it locally, using an on-device page-reading model and translation model (“Best” mode), which are downloaded once, and Google ML Kit on-device translation (“Fast” mode).
  • Screen capture. SubInk uses Android’s screen capture (MediaProjection) to see the page you are reading. Capture runs only while the floating button session is on. Android asks for your consent every session, and you can stop it at any time from the notification. Page images are processed in memory on the device only. They are never saved and never sent to us or to any third party.
  • Your glossary and settings are stored only on your device. You can clear them in the app or by clearing the app’s data.

2. Data collected by third-party services

We don't operate our own analytics or ad servers and don't sell personal data. The following Google services are built into the app. Their data is linked to app or device identifiers, never to your name or account.

ServiceDataPurpose
Firebase Crashlytics Crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, Android version, app version and a Crashlytics installation ID. Never page images or page content. Find and fix crashes
Google Analytics for Firebase App usage events and an app instance ID. Never page text or images. Understand which features and languages are used
Google AdMob (only while ads are enabled) Advertising ID, device information, IP address, ad interactions. Ads may be personalised only with your consent. Show ads
Google User Messaging Platform (only while ads are enabled) Your consent choices, where a consent form is required (for example in the EEA and the UK). Record ad and privacy choices

These services act under Google's terms and policies: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps that use its services · Firebase privacy.

About ads: SubInk may show ads. Ads are shown only inside the app’s own screens, never over other apps. Ads are currently turned off. While they are off, the ads service isn't started and collects nothing.

3. When the app uses the internet

  • Model downloads: the translation and page-reading models are downloaded once, in the background on first launch (Wi‑Fi only by default), through Google Play or directly from Hugging Face or GitHub hosts.
  • ML Kit language packs: Google ML Kit downloads language packs from Google once, the first time a language is used in Fast mode.
  • App configuration: the app downloads a small settings file. It contains no personal data and the app sends nothing about you beyond the standard web request; like any web request, the server sees your IP address, which we don't use or store.
  • Crash reports, usage statistics and, while enabled, ads (section 2).

Reading and translating pages works offline once the models are installed.

4. Permissions

  • Display over other apps: to draw the floating button and the translations on top of your reader app.
  • Notifications: an ongoing notification while a session is running, with Pause and Stop actions.
  • Screen capture consent (MediaProjection) with a foreground service: Android asks for your consent every session. The service runs only while the session is on.
  • Foreground service for model downloads: keeps a model download running in the background.
  • Internet and network state: for the downloads, ads and reporting in section 3.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID / Ad Services): used for ads while they are enabled.

SubInk does not use an Accessibility Service and does not request storage, camera, microphone or location permissions. Glossary CSV files are imported and exported only through the system file picker that you choose.

5. Your choices and data deletion

  • Screen capture: stop a session at any time from the notification. Capture needs your consent again for each new session.
  • Ads (while enabled): you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads). Where a consent form applies, you can change your choice again in the app.
  • Data on your device: edit or delete glossary entries in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
  • Data held by Google services: it isn't linked to your identity. To ask us to delete analytics or crash data related to your device, email us (section 9) with the approximate date. We'll handle it through the tools these services provide.

6. Children

SubInk is a general translation tool and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we'll take appropriate steps.
