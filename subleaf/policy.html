1. What stays on your device

  • Translation runs on your device. SubLeaf has two quality modes: “Best”, which uses an on-device AI translation model that is downloaded once and then runs locally, and “Fast”, which also translates on the device.
  • The books you open (EPUB and TXT), their translations, your edits, your glossary and your reading progress are processed and stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are not sent to us or to any third party, and the app does not back them up to the cloud.
  • Files are read only when you pick them or share them to the app. When you save, SubLeaf writes a new file to Downloads/SubLeaf or to a folder you choose. It never overwrites the original.
  • Your settings and the books you added are stored only on your device. Deleting a book in the app removes it and its translation, glossary and reading progress. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stores.

2. Data collected by third-party services

We don't operate our own analytics or ad servers and don't sell personal data. The following Google services are built into the app. Their data is linked to app or device identifiers, never to your name or account.

These services act under Google's terms and policies: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps that use its services · Firebase privacy.

About ads: SubLeaf is free and may show ads to fund its development.

3. When the app uses the internet

  • Translation model and language packs: downloaded once in the background. You can choose to download over Wi-Fi only.
  • App configuration: the app downloads a small remote settings file. It sends nothing about you beyond the standard web request; like any web request, the server sees your IP address, which we don't use or store.
  • Google Play: in-app update and review prompts, and model delivery through Play Asset Delivery.
  • Ads and Firebase crash reports and usage statistics (section 2).

Your books are never sent over the internet.

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for the downloads, ads and reporting in section 3, and for the Wi-Fi-only download option.
  • Notifications: shows the progress of a translation.
  • Foreground service (data sync): keeps a long translation running while the app is in the background.
  • Run at startup: used only to show a “tap to continue” notification after a restart. It does not start translating by itself.
  • Advertising ID: used for ads.

SubLeaf does not request storage, location or contacts permissions, and has no account. Files are read only when you pick or share them, and saved only to Downloads/SubLeaf or a folder you choose.

5. Your choices and data deletion

  • Ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads). Where a consent form applies, you can change your choice again in the app.
  • Data on your device: delete a book in the app to remove it with its translation, glossary and reading progress, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Files you saved to Downloads or another folder are yours and stay until you delete them.
  • Data held by Google services: it isn't linked to your identity. To ask us to delete analytics or crash data related to your device, email us (section 9) with the approximate date. We'll handle it through the tools these services provide.

6. Children

SubLeaf is a general translation tool and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we'll take appropriate steps.
