1. What stays on your device

  • Text recognition (OCR) and translation run entirely on your device. SubGlance uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition (on-device) and the Tencent HY-MT translation model, which is downloaded once and then runs locally through llama.cpp.
  • Screen content captured for the Subtitles and Screen area features, camera frames, photos you pick, text you type or share to the app, and all translations are processed in memory on your device. They are not sent to us or to any third party.
  • Your settings, glossary and translation history are stored only on your device. You can clear them in the app or by clearing the app's data.
  • Voice-over and "Read aloud" use your phone's text-to-speech engine with offline voices.

2. Data collected by third-party services

We don't operate our own analytics or ad servers and don't sell personal data. The following Google services are built into the app. Their data is linked to app or device identifiers, never to your name or account.

ServiceDataPurpose
Firebase Crashlytics Crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, Android version, app version, app state when the error happened (for example which feature was open and the selected language codes), and a Crashlytics installation ID. No screen content or text. Find and fix crashes
Google Analytics for Firebase App interaction events (for example which feature was opened or started, and which language was picked), app instance ID, device and app information, approximate region derived from the IP address. Understand which features and languages are used
Google AdMob (only while ads are enabled) Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions. Ads may be personalised where allowed and where you've agreed. Show ads that fund the app
Google User Messaging Platform (only while ads are enabled) Your consent choices, where a consent message is required in your region. Record ad and privacy choices

These services act under Google's terms and policies: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps that use its services · Firebase privacy. Data is encrypted in transit and kept for the retention periods those services use.

About ads: SubGlance may show ads to fund its development. Ads can be turned on or off without an app update. While they are off, the ads service isn't started and collects nothing. When they are on, ads appear only inside the app's own screens, never over other apps or your videos.

3. When the app uses the internet

  • Translation model download: once, through Google Play (or a direct download if Play delivery isn't available).
  • App configuration: the app periodically downloads a small settings file from our server to turn features on or off. It sends nothing about you; like any web request, the server sees your IP address, which we don't use or store.
  • Language packs and voices may be downloaded by Google Play services or your phone's text-to-speech engine when needed.
  • Crash reports, usage statistics and, while enabled, ads (section 2).

Translation itself never needs the internet.

4. Permissions

  • Display over other apps: shows the translation box, the screen-area frame and the floating controls on top of other apps.
  • Screen capture (asked each time a session starts): reads the part of the screen you chose, so Subtitles and Screen area can recognise its text. Captured frames are processed on the device and discarded.
  • Camera: only for Camera translate. Frames are processed live on the device. A frozen shot is saved to your gallery only if you turn on that setting.
  • Notifications: shows the ongoing notification with the controls of a running translation session.
  • Photos: you pick an image through Android's photo picker, which doesn't give the app access to your other photos.
  • Internet: see section 3.

5. Your choices and data deletion

  • Ads (while enabled): you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads). Where a consent message applies, you can change your choice anytime in the app under Settings › About › Privacy options.
  • Data on your device: clear the history and glossary in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
  • Data held by Google services: it isn't linked to your identity. To ask us to delete analytics or crash data related to your device, email us (section 10) with the approximate date. We'll handle it through the tools these services provide.

6. Children

SubGlance is a general translation tool and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we'll take appropriate steps.
