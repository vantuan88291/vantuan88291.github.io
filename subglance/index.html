How it works

Ready in three steps

Download a language

Get the translation model once. After that it lives on your phone.

Pick a mode

Subtitles, screen area, text or camera, whichever fits what you're looking at.

Read it in your language

Privacy

Private by design

  • On-device. Recognition and translation run on your phone. Your text, screen and photos are never uploaded.
  • No account. No sign-up and no sign-in.
  • Minimal network use. The app uses the internet only for the one-time model download, app settings updates, and anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. See the Privacy Policy.
